# Atelier Libr'Émotions — Brand context

Atelier Libr'Émotions presents Véronique Berthier's intuitive painting, ceramics and creative practice.
The artwork and the artist's own words are the primary evidence for its identity.
This guide records confirmed repository content separately from design direction; it does not introduce a new brand or approve a redesign.

Confirm factual changes against the request and current content before publishing them.

## Intended character

Keep the presentation warm, personal, calm and expressive.
Let intuitive marks, color, texture and ceramic form supply the personality.
Use clear contemporary navigation and readable copy to make the work easy to explore.

| Theme             | Expression in the site                                                            | Boundary                                                                |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Emotion           | Artist-authored subtitles, artwork descriptions and restrained expressive accents | Avoid exaggerated promises or invented interpretations                  |
| Material          | Complete artwork, useful detail views and confirmed technique labels              | Do not flatten every collection into interchangeable cards              |
| Nature            | Existing artwork and the artist's stated inspiration                              | Avoid replacing the portfolio with generic foliage or stock photography |
| Personal practice | First-person artistic approach and a recognizable artist identity                 | Do not turn personal experience into a clinical or therapeutic claim    |

The existing cream surfaces, dark text and metallic accents support this direction.
They are implemented design choices, not evidence of a separately approved brand palette.
Use [design-system.md](design-system.md) for the current tokens and styling ownership.

## Assets and identity boundaries

Preserve the supplied logo and original artwork.
Do not crop, recolor, generate replacements for or rename artist assets as part of routine layout or documentation work.
Use decorative treatments sparingly and keep them subordinate to the work.
Social previews may be cropped by platforms; gallery and viewer presentation should preserve the artwork's composition.

Use [content-direction.md](content-direction.md) for voice, titles and factual boundaries.
Ground additions in the artist's confirmed practice and existing visual identity.
